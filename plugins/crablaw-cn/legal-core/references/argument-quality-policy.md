# Argument and Quality Policy

Run these checks after issue-level analysis and before the writer receives findings.

## Traceability

- Every claim maps to legal elements.
- Every element maps to fact/evidence IDs and legal source IDs, or is explicitly missing.
- Every citation tag agrees with the status and type of its source record.
- Every case comparison identifies material similarities and differences.

## Adversarial review

For each material issue, identify:

- the strongest contrary rule or interpretation;
- the strongest contrary fact/evidence reading;
- admissibility/authenticity/relevance weaknesses;
- jurisdiction, timing, limitation, remedy, and procedure defenses;
- an alternative/retreat position where appropriate.

Do not invent an opponent's evidence. Label hypothetical counterarguments as hypotheses.

## Logic checks

Reject or flag:

- a conclusion that merely restates a premise;
- an unstated middle premise;
- affirming the consequent or denying the antecedent;
- inconsistent party, date, amount, version, or issue IDs;
- selection of only favorable cases without disclosing contrary authority;
- a precise probability or outcome prediction unsupported by a defined dataset.

## Severity and confidence

- Severity measures decision impact/urgency, not the model's certainty.
- Confidence describes support quality and states its basis.
- Missing decisive facts or unverified controlling law prevents high confidence.
- An upstream severity may be downgraded only with a recorded reason and reviewer-visible history.

## Writer gate

The writer receives only schema-valid findings. It may organize and explain them, but may not add a
new fact, source, conclusion, recommendation, or severity. New substance returns to the analyzer.
