# Legal Reasoning Modes

Use one or more modes only after facts, issues, and sources are structured. Record the selected mode
in the finding confidence basis so a reviewer can reproduce the path.

## Deductive application

Structure rule → elements/exceptions → supported facts → conclusion. A formally valid chain does not
repair a false or stale premise; keep premise validity and logical validity separate.

## Interpretation

Start with text and defined terms, then system/context, purpose, history when reliable, and conflict
rules. State competing interpretations and why one is stronger. Do not exceed the text's defensible
range without naming the gap.

## Inductive practice summary

Infer a practice tendency only from a disclosed case set. State sampling limits, jurisdiction/time
bias, contrary cases, and why the pattern may not generalize.

## Analogical reasoning

Identify the legally relevant similarity and material differences. Analogy is restricted or
unavailable where legality and statutory reservation demand a clear rule, including criminal
liability and taxation. Escalate uncertain use to a lawyer.

## Abductive hypothesis

Generate competing explanations for incomplete evidence, list predicted observations, and seek
disconfirming evidence. A best-current explanation remains a hypothesis, not an established fact.

## Counterfactual causation

State the changed fact, hold other material conditions explicit, compare outcomes, and distinguish
factual causation from legal attribution and scope of responsibility.

## Conflict resolution

Classify the conflict first: authority, time, territory, special/general rule, evidence, or issue
priority. Apply controlling hierarchy before discretionary balancing. Preserve unresolved conflicts
as lawyer-review items.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- no result prediction from model memory;
- no legal consequence without cited rule and mapped facts;
- no confidence score without a written basis;
- no silent change from disputed allegation to established fact;
- no silent downgrade of an upstream RED/YELLOW finding.
